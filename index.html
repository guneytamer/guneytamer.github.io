      About

      Turkey → Germany → Boston → LA.

      A classically trained musician who fell for electronic music in Germany, then followed it to Berklee and on to Los Angeles.

      Since 2018 Güney has designed sound for short films, animation and games, edited sound libraries at Sounddogs, scored commercials and mastered records for other artists.

      Today he runs Black Lotus Recording Studios in Burbank and Mobius Audio, and looks for projects with a strong artistic personality and an experimental streak — work where sound carries meaning, not just atmosphere.
